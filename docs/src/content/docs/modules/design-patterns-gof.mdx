## 12. Common Mistakes & Anti-Patterns

Avoid these common traps frequently encountered by junior and mid-level developers:

- ⚠️ **Skipping fundamentals and jumping straight into high-level abstractions.**
- ⚠️ **Ignoring error states, nullability, and resource leaks.**
- ⚠️ **Failing to profile performance under realistic concurrency or data volume.**

## 1. Why Mental Translation Causes Hesitation

When you think in your native language, translate grammar, look up vocabulary, and then speak, your brain experiences **cognitive bottlenecking** (~2–4 seconds of awkward silence).

:::tip The 3-Level "Think in English" Workout
1. **Level 1 (Naming Objects)**: Look around your desk right now. Name items directly in English (*monitor, keyboard, notebook, mug*) without naming them in your native language first.
2. **Level 2 (Simple Narratives)**: Silently describe what you are doing in simple present tense: *"I am opening the editor. I am checking the logs. The terminal looks clean."*
3. **Level 3 (Reasoning & Debate)**: Talk through pros and cons out loud: *"I could use a hash map here, but if memory is tight, an array might be sufficient."*
:::

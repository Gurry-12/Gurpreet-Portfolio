## 1. Safe Small Talk Topics (The 3 Ws)

- **Weekend**: *"Any fun plans for the weekend?"* / *"How was your weekend? Did you get some rest?"*
- **Weather / Location**: *"How is the weather over there today? Still raining?"*
- **Work / Week**: *"How's your week shaping up?"* / *"Busy sprint on your side?"*

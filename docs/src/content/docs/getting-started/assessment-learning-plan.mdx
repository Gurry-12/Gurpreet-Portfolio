## 🎯 1. Rapid Self-Assessment Diagnostic (CEFR Benchmark)

Answer these questions honestly to identify your baseline proficiency level:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Stage 1: A2 (Elementary) -> B1 (Intermediate)" icon="star">
    * **Can you explain yesterday's work in 3 sentences during a standup?**
    * **Can you write a concise bug ticket with reproduction steps?**
    * ⚠️ *Common Roadblocks*: Pausing frequently to translate from native language, mixing Simple Past (*"I did not went"*) and Present Continuous (*"I am knowing"*).
    * **Primary Focus**: Verb tenses in action, eliminating L1 habit transfers.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stage 2: B1 (Intermediate) -> B2 (Professional)" icon="rocket">
    * **Can you debate an architectural trade-off (e.g. SQL vs NoSQL) without hesitation?**
    * **Can you politely push back on unrealistic sprint deadlines?**
    * ⚠️ *Common Roadblocks*: Overly formal textbook phrases (*"Kindly do the needful"*), missing syllable stress on technical terms (*'COM-po-nent* vs *com-PO-nent*).
    * **Primary Focus**: Natural connected speech, discourse markers, STAR method behavioral storytelling.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## 📅 2. The 90-Day Deliberate Practice Roadmap

<Steps>
1. **Month 1 (Days 1–30): Structural Precision & Pronunciation Reset**
   - **Daily Time**: 20 minutes (10m Grammar in Action + 10m IPA / Connected Speech).
   - **Goal**: Master the 4 core professional tenses (Simple Past, Present Perfect, Present Continuous, Future Intent).
   - **Key Lesson**: [Tenses Masterclass](/docs/grammar/tenses-masterclass/) & [IPA for Indian Speakers](/docs/pronunciation/sounds-and-ipa-guide/).

2. **Month 2 (Days 31–60): Workplace Communication & Spontaneous Fluency**
   - **Daily Time**: 25 minutes (15m Speaking drills + 10m Email/RFC writing).
   - **Goal**: Deliver crisp, impact-driven Agile standups and sprint demo walkthroughs.
   - **Key Lesson**: [Agile Standups & Status Updates](/docs/professional/standups-and-status-updates/) & [Fluency Signposting](/docs/speaking/fluency-and-signposting/).

3. **Month 3 (Days 61–90): High-Stakes Technical Debates & Interview Mastery**
   - **Daily Time**: 30 minutes (20m STAR mock interviews + 10m Architectural debates).
   - **Goal**: Confidently handle system design grilling, executive summaries, and cross-team negotiation.
   - **Key Lesson**: [Interview English (STAR Method)](/docs/professional/interview-english-star-method/) & [Constructive Feedback](/docs/professional/constructive-feedback/).
</Steps>

## 1. What is Natural Language Acquisition?

Traditional language learning treats English as a subject to be **memorized** (memorizing grammar rules, translation exercises, vocabulary lists). 

:::tip The Acquisition Mental Model (Stephen Krashen)
You do not acquire language fluency by studying grammar rules in isolation. You acquire language through **Comprehensible Input** (listening and reading content you understand at 80%–90%) paired with **Immediate Output & Feedback** (speaking and writing).
:::

## 2. The 4 Golden Pillars of Daily English Mastery

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="1. Stop Translating Word-by-Word" icon="random">
    When you translate from your native language (*"Mujhe ek doubt hai"* \to *"I am having a doubt"*), you produce unnatural sentences. **Map concepts directly to English expressions** (*"I have a question"*).
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Speak Out Loud Every Single Day" icon="comment">
    Reading silently trains your eyes, not your vocal cords or tongue muscles. Set a 3-minute timer daily and speak out loud to build muscle memory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Learn Words in Collocations" icon="open-book">
    Never memorize isolated words. Learn word partnerships: not just *overhead*, but **incur overhead**; not just *risk*, but **mitigate risk**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Track Mistakes in an Error Log" icon="list-format">
    Mistakes are the highest-value learning data you possess. Log what you said, why it was unnatural, and the target phrasing.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## 3. Daily 15-Minute Deliberate Practice Routine

<Steps>
1. **Minute 1–5 (Input & Collocations)**: Read 1 lesson in this knowledge base; identify 3 new collocations or sentence structures.
1. **Minute 6–10 (Active Speaking Drill)**: Pick a prompt from the [Daily Practice](/docs/practice/daily-30-day-routine/) section. Speak out loud for 3 minutes without pausing or using filler words.
1. **Minute 11–13 (Written Reflection)**: Write 3 crisp sentences summarizing what you spoke about.
1. **Minute 14–15 (Error Log Update)**: Review any hesitations or grammar doubts in your [Personal Error Log](/docs/practice/error-log-system/).
</Steps>

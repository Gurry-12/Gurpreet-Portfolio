## 1. The 8 Parts of Speech Quick-Reference

Every word in English plays a specific functional role in a sentence:

| Part of Speech | Functional Role | Technical / Workplace Examples | Example in a Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Noun** | Names a person, place, system, or concept | *server, database, latency, architecture* | *"The **database** handles millions of transactions."* |
| **2. Pronoun** | Replaces a noun to prevent repetition | *it, they, we, who, which* | *"The pod restarted because **it** ran out of memory."* |
| **3. Verb** | Expresses action, occurrence, or state of being | *deploy, compile, optimize, is, have* | *"We **optimized** the search queries."* |
| **4. Adjective** | Modifies or describes a noun | *scalable, redundant, unindexed, fast* | *"We built a **scalable** microservice."* |
| **5. Adverb** | Modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb | *efficiently, periodically, extremely* | *"The system scales **efficiently** under load."* |
| **6. Preposition** | Shows relationship in time, place, or direction | *in, on, at, with, during, between* | *"The cache resides **in** memory."* |
| **7. Conjunction**| Connects words, phrases, or clauses | *and, but, because, although, whereas* | *"The query is fast, **but** it requires high RAM."* |
| **8. Interjection**| Expresses sudden emotion | *well, oh, indeed, absolutely* | *"**Well**, that approach solves the problem."* |

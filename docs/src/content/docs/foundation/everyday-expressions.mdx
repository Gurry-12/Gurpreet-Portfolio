## 1. Natural Greetings & Responses

| Situation | Stiff / Textbook Phrase (Avoid) | Natural Conversational Phrasing (Use) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Morning Meeting Greeting** | *"Respected sir, good morning to one and all."* | *"Good morning everyone, hope you're having a good week."* |
| **Responding to "How are you?"**| *"I am fine. What about you?"* | *"Doing well, thanks! How are things with you?"* |
| **Thanking a Colleague** | *"Thanks and regards for the same."* | *"Thanks a lot for your help on this, really appreciate it!"* |
| **Apologizing for a Delay** | *"Sorry for the inconvenience caused."* | *"Sorry for the delay in getting back to you."* |
| **Interrupting Politely** | *"Excuse me, stop please."* | *"Sorry to jump in, but could I quickly ask a question?"* |

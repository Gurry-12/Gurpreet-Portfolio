## 1. Meeting Leader Phrases

- **Opening**: *"Thanks for joining everyone. Let's get started. Our goal today is to align on the Q3 roadmap."*
- **Keeping on Track**: *"In the interest of time, let's take that discussion offline and stick to the agenda."*
- **Action Items**: *"To wrap up: Amit will finish the API spec by Wednesday, and Sarah will review by Friday."*

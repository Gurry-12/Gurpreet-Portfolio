## 1. The STAR Breakdown

- **Situation (15%)**: *"At my last job, our payment service experienced latency spikes during flash sales."*
- **Task (15%)**: *"My task was to identify the root cause and ensure 99.99% availability."*
- **Action (50%)**: *"I profiled the application, identified N+1 database queries, added composite indexes, and implemented a Redis caching layer."*
- **Result (20%)**: *"As a result, p99 latency dropped from 2.5s to 45ms, and the system handled 10,000 requests per second with zero errors."*

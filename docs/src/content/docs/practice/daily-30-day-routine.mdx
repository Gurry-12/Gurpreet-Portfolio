## 1. 30-Day Speaking Workout Calendar

- **Day 1**: Explain how your favorite web app works in 2 minutes out loud.
- **Day 2**: Narrate a difficult decision you made last year using the 3rd conditional (*"If I had..."*).
- **Day 3**: Deliver a 60-second self-introduction for a dream job interview.
- **Day 4**: Practice polite disagreement with a simulated proposal to rewrite your entire codebase.
- **Day 5**: Describe your morning routine using connected speech and linking.
- **Day 6**: Explain the difference between two competing technologies out loud.
- **Day 7**: Give a simulated postmortem update for a major server outage.

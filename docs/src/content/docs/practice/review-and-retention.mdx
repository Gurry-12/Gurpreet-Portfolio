## 1. The Weekly Spaced Review Protocol

- **End of Day**: Spend 2 minutes reading through today's new collocations out loud.
- **End of Week (Sunday)**: Filter your [Error Log](/docs/practice/error-log-system/) for items marked `🔄 Review`. Speak 3 original sentences for each logged error.
- **End of Month**: Record a 5-minute presentation on any topic; listen to the recording and grade your pronunciation, pauses, and word choices.

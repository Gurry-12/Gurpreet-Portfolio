## 1. High-Value Academic & Professional Words

- **Facilitate (Verb)**: To make an action or process easier (*"The new dashboard facilitates real-time monitoring"*).
- **Substantiate (Verb)**: To provide evidence to support a claim (*"We need data to substantiate this architectural proposal"*).
- **Viable (Adjective)**: Capable of working successfully (*"Serverless is a viable solution for our bursty traffic profile"*).
- **Comprehensive (Adjective)**: Complete and including all elements (*"We conducted a comprehensive security audit"*).
- **Discrepancy (Noun)**: An illogical difference between facts or numbers (*"There is a discrepancy between the report and the raw logs"*).

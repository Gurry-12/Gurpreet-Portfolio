## 1. Strategic & Business Collocations

- **Reach a consensus** (*"After discussing the trade-offs, the team reached a consensus."*)
- **Mitigate risk** (*"Automated testing helps mitigate the risk of production regressions."*)
- **Streamline processes** (*"CI/CD pipelines streamline our deployment processes."*)
- **Incur overhead** (*"Adding unnecessary layers incurs computational overhead."*)
- **Bridge the gap** (*"This workshop helped bridge the gap between product and engineering."*)
- **Meet a deadline** (*"We worked collaboratively to meet the strict project deadline."*)
- **Raise an objection** (*"Nobody raised any objections to the proposed architecture."*)

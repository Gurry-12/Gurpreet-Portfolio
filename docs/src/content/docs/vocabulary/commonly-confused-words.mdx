## 1. Key Confusion Pairs

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Say vs Tell" icon="random">
    - **Say (+ words)**: *"He **said** that the meeting was postponed."* (No direct personal pronoun).
    - **Tell (+ person + words)**: *"He **told me** that the meeting was postponed."* (Requires personal object!).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Make vs Do" icon="puzzle">
    - **Make (Creating/Producing)**: *Make a decision, make a mistake, make progress, make an announcement.*
    - **Do (Actions/Tasks)**: *Do homework, do research, do a favor, do your best, do business.*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Advice vs Advise" icon="document">
    - **Advice (Noun - /s/)**: *"He gave me great **advice**."*
    - **Advise (Verb - /z/)**: *"I **advise** you to review the contract carefully."*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fewer vs Less" icon="list-format">
    - **Fewer (Countable nouns)**: *"We had **fewer** bugs this sprint."*
    - **Less (Uncountable nouns)**: *"The new architecture consumes **less** memory."*
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

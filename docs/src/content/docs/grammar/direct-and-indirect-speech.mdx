## 1. Backshift Rules in Reported Speech

When reporting past conversations, verbs shift back one tense:

- *"The client said: 'We **need** the report today.'"* (Direct)
  \to *"The client said that they **needed** the report that day."* (Reported)
- *"Rahul said: 'I **have deployed** the service.'"*
  \to *"Rahul mentioned that he **had deployed** the service."*
- *"Sarah asked: 'Can you finish by 5 PM?'"*
  \to *"Sarah asked if I **could** finish by 5 PM."*

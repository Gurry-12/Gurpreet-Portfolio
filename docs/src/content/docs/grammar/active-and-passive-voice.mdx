## 1. When to Use Active vs Passive Voice

- **Use Active Voice (Preferred 80% of the time)**: Clear ownership, dynamism, and directness.
  - *"I optimized the search query."* (Clear who did the work).
- **Use Passive Voice (Technical docs, incident reports, when actor is unknown or irrelevant)**:
  - *"The payment service **was restarted** at 14:00 UTC."* (Focus is on the service, not the person who typed the command).
  - *"User passwords **are hashed** using bcrypt."* (Standard security process).

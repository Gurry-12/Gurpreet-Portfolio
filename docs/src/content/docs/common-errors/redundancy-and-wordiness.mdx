## 1. Common Redundancies to Cut

- ❌ *"Return back"* \to ✅ **Return**
- ❌ *"Repeat again"* \to ✅ **Repeat**
- ❌ *"End result"* \to ✅ **Result**
- ❌ *"Due to the fact that"* \to ✅ **Because**
- ❌ *"At this point in time"* \to ✅ **Now / Currently**
- ❌ *"In order to"* \to ✅ **To**

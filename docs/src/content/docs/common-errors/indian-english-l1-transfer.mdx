## 1. Top 8 Corrections

| Common Regional Phrase | Natural Global Alternative | Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"I am having a doubt."* | *"I **have a question**."* | "Doubt" means suspicion or mistrust in standard English. |
| *"Let's discuss about the plan."* | *"Let's **discuss the plan**."* | "Discuss" is a transitive verb taking a direct object. |
| *"Kindly revert back."* | *"Please **reply / get back to me**."* | "Revert" means restore/return; "back" is redundant. |
| *"I passed out in 2024."* | *"I **graduated** in 2024."* | "Pass out" means faint / lose consciousness. |
| *"Can we prepone the call?"* | *"Can we **move the call up**?"* | "Prepone" is unknown outside South Asia. |
| *"I cannot cope up with this."* | *"I cannot **cope with** this."* | Correct idiom is **cope with**. |
| *"He is out of station."* | *"He is **out of town / away**."* | Colonial railway phrase; obsolete internationally. |
| *"Today morning / Yesterday night"*| *"**This morning / Last night**"* | Literal translation of *"Aaj subah"* / *"Kal raat"*. |

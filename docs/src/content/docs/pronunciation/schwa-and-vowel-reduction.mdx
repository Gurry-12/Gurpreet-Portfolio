## 1. What is the Schwa `/ə/`?

The **Schwa `/ə/`** is the ultimate lazy vowel. Your lips, tongue, and jaw are completely relaxed in the center of your mouth, making a brief "uh" sound.

Over **30% of all vowel sounds** in spoken English are reduced to the Schwa:

- **About**: `/əˈbaʊt/` (The 'a' is not "ah", it is "uh").
- **Computer**: `/kəmˈpjuː.tər/` (The 'o' is "uh").
- **Banana**: `/bəˈnɑː.nə/` (First and last 'a' are both Schwa).
- **Today**: `/təˈdeɪ/` (Not "too-day", but "tuh-day").
